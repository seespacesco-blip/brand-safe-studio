AI Governance & Approval Policy

AI is Permitted To:

  • Generate initial creative drafts across multiple formats (Social, Email, Visual).
  • Adapt brand tone based on target audience.
  • Scan human edits in real-time for compliance risks.
  • Suggest fixes for flagged claims based on ground truth.

Human Accountability Required:

  • Factual Verification: AI output must be checked against the product factsheet.
  • Final Sign-Off: AI cannot publish directly. A human must click "Approve".
  • Override Authority: Only humans can rewrite flagged content to ensure strict legal compliance.
